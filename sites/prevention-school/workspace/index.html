Security & Law

Absolute Student Data Protection

No leaks. We guarantee compliance with personal data laws and psychologist-client confidentiality through our "Local Vault" architecture.

Workspace operates without transmitting names to the internet.

  • Everything is stored on your PC: Names, contacts, and personal student cards are encrypted and physically located only on the psychologist's work computer.
  • Anonymization for AI: If the psychologist asks the AI assistant for advice, all names and identifiable details are automatically erased before analysis.
  • The principal sees numbers, not secrets: Management receives stats like "15 anxiety requests in grade 8B," but doesn't see who made them.
  • Works offline: Logs and cards are always available, even if the school network goes down.
