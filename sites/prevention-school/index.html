Security & Sovereignty

Designed for the Public Sector

An architecture that alleviates fears of local regulatory bodies and data leaks. Psychologists work in a secure perimeter, and the system is fully ready to transition to sovereign on-premise AI models.

🛡

Local-First Architecture (HIPAA/GDPR)

The database (SQLCipher) is physically stored on the psychologist's encrypted drive, not in the cloud. Names, schools, or addresses are never sent to the neural network — only anonymized behavioral patterns. AI works exclusively as a drafting assistant; the final decision is always made by a human.

🏛

Technological Sovereignty

The infrastructure is LLM-agnostic and currently powered by advanced models like Google Gemini. We are open to strategic partnerships to deploy secure Enterprise AI environments, creating a reliable standard for psychologists' digital workspaces in high-security institutions.

⚡

Deployment Without IT

Centers and schools do not need their own servers or DevOps engineers. The psychologist's terminal is downloaded and installed like a regular desktop app. The director views aggregated analytics through a browser. Seamless integration in 1 working day.

System Evolution

The Evolution of Adolescent Prevention Systems

The "Prevention-AI" platform was created to eliminate systemic gaps and bottlenecks in the public infrastructure for psychological support and prevention of deviations among adolescents. We transform fragmented processes into a single, manageable, and evidence-based ecosystem.

1

Unified Methodology vs Fragmented Approaches

⚠ Challenge

The traditional system lacks a unified vision. Participants in prevention (officials, theorists, practitioners from different agencies) often speak different languages and use uncoordinated tools.

✓ Platform Solution

All products — from specialist workspaces to family apps — run on a shared knowledge base with end-to-end metrics. The built-in "Academy" upskills specialists, forming a unified standard of work at all levels of prevention.

2

Transparent Telemetry vs Blind Spots in Reports

⚠ Challenge

Standard departmental reporting does not reflect the real picture. It fails to capture the operational level or answer questions about the actual workload of a school psychologist.

✓ Platform Solution

Advanced non-personalized telemetry. The system automatically generates detailed statistical arrays about specialists' work. Managers get accurate data on reach, workload, and request themes.

3

"Evidence Factory" vs Intuitive Methods

⚠ Challenge

In practical psychology, it has historically been difficult to collect an objective evidence base for the effectiveness of applied methods and programs.

✓ Platform Solution

Creating a continuous "evidence factory." Through big data analysis, AI algorithms constantly evaluate the effectiveness of preventive protocols, forming a reliable, scientifically grounded base for specialists.

4

Automated Macro-Analytics vs Expensive Research

⚠ Challenge

Collecting any reliable epidemiological data on the state of adolescents in a region is a long, complex, and extremely expensive process.

✓ Platform Solution

Seamless data aggregation. The platform collects massive amounts of anonymized data in the background at no extra cost. This information is instantly reflected in Territorial Dashboards, giving authorities a real-time heat map of risks.

5

Technological Equality vs Geographical Barriers

⚠ Challenge

Today, the quality of a psychologist's work heavily depends on their geography. Access to advanced methods, supervision, and updates is vastly different between major cities and remote areas.

✓ Platform Solution

100% equal access. The platform provides the most advanced resources and AI tools to all specialists without exception — whether they work in a metropolitan center or a rural school.

6

Real Human Connection vs Paper Routine

⚠ Challenge

Specialists responsible for children's health are overwhelmed by bureaucracy. A huge portion of their time is spent filling out paperwork instead of helping adolescents.

✓ Platform Solution

The AI assistant takes over the routine. Automatic filling of logs and generation of plans and reports frees up the psychologist's time for what they entered the profession to do: real human interaction.
