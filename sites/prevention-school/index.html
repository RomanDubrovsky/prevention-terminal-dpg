Open Teenology About the project
System Evolution

Evolution of the teenage deviance prevention system

The Prevention-AI platform was created to eliminate systemic gaps and bottlenecks in the state infrastructure of psychological support and deviance prevention among teenagers. We turn fragmented processes into a single, manageable, and scientifically based ecosystem.

1

Unified methodology instead of fragmented approaches

⚠ Challenge

The traditional system lacks a unified vision. Participants in prevention (officials, theorists, practitioners from different agencies) often speak different languages and use uncoordinated tools.

✓ Platform Solution

All products — from specialists' workspaces to apps for families — run on a common knowledge base with an end-to-end indicator system. The base is easily updated following scientific innovations or changes in laws. The built-in "Academy" improves specialists' qualifications, forming a unified standard of work at all levels of prevention.

2

Transparent telemetry instead of "blind spots" in reports

⚠ Challenge

Standard departmental reporting does not reflect the real picture. It does not affect the operational level and does not answer the question about the real workload of a school psychologist.

✓ Platform Solution

Advanced non-personalized telemetry. The system automatically generates detailed statistical arrays about specialists' work. Managers receive accurate data on coverage, workload, and themes of requests, which significantly exceeds the capabilities of standard reports.

3

"Evidence factory" instead of intuitive methods

⚠ Challenge

In practical psychology, it is historically difficult to collect an objective evidence base for the effectiveness of applied methods and programs.

✓ Platform Solution

Creation of a continuous "evidence factory". Thanks to big data analysis, AI algorithms constantly evaluate the effectiveness of prevention protocols, forming a reliable scientifically sound basis for specialists' work.

4

Automatic macro-analytics instead of expensive research

⚠ Challenge

Collecting any reliable epidemiological data on the state of teenagers in a region is a long, complex, and extremely expensive process.

✓ Platform Solution

Seamless data aggregation. The platform collects massive arrays of anonymized data in the background without additional costs. This information is instantly reflected in Territorial Dashboards, giving authorities an up-to-date heat map of risks from the level of an individual school to an entire region.

5

Technological equality instead of geographical barriers

⚠ Challenge

Today, the quality of a psychologist's work heavily depends on their geography. Access to advanced methods, supervisions, and updates in the capital and remote areas is incomparable.

✓ Platform Solution

100% equal access. The platform provides the most modern resources and AI tools to all specialists without exception — regardless of whether they work in the center of a metropolis or a rural school.

6

Live work with people instead of paper routine

⚠ Challenge

Specialists responsible for children's health are overloaded with bureaucracy. A huge part of their time is spent filling out papers, not helping teenagers.

✓ Platform Solution

AI assistant takes over the routine. Automatic filling of journals, generation of individual basic plans and reports frees up the psychologist's time for what they came to the profession for — for live work with people.

Governance and Sustainability

Financially sustainable open-source infrastructure.

100% Free Core

Electronic workspaces

The core infrastructure — the CRM for educational psychologists, document management tools, analytical dashboards for school principals and government specialists — is 100% free and open-source. This ensures that every specialist and educational institution has access to modern tools and training without financial burden or vendor lock-in.

Subscription

AI Analytics

Advanced AI assistants for case analysis and drafting documents in real-time operate on a paid subscription. This model directly covers ongoing costs for LLM tokens and cloud computing, making the open infrastructure self-sustaining.
