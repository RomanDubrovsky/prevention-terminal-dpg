🎙️ Voice Intake & Socratic Copilot

The built-in AI copilot acts as an intelligent administrative exoskeleton, handling paperwork and providing structured on-the-spot clinical guardrails.

How 30-Second Voice Intake Works:

1. Tap the microphone button immediately after finishing a consultation session.

2. Speak for 30–60 seconds in plain language: student concerns, core discussion points, and agreed action steps.

3. The model strips conversational filler, redacts personal identifiers, and structures the note into formal clinical headings.

On-Demand Methodological Support:

When encountering difficult or ambiguous situations, the copilot offers evidence-based Socratic questions and guidance:

  • How to provide psychological first aid when a student is in tears or shutdown mode;
  • De-escalation dialogue frameworks for sudden behavioral outbursts in class;
  • Structured triage algorithms for bullying, peer isolation, and school refusal;
  • Constructive talking points for tense parent conferences.

🔒 Privacy & Regulatory Safeguards

Protecting student confidentiality is paramount. Prevention School enforces privacy at the architectural level rather than as an afterthought.

1. Zero-Knowledge Local Encrypted Vault:

Student names, contact details, and private counselor session notes reside strictly in an encrypted database on the counselor's computer. Without the master decryption key, data cannot be extracted even if the physical drive is accessed.

2. Client-Side Anonymization Before AI Inference:

When using voice intake or methodological suggestions, client-side regex and NER filters strip all names, birthdates, addresses, and class numbers. The AI model only receives anonymized clinical context (e.g., "14-year-old student, peer conflict, test anxiety").

3. Strict Role Separation:

School administrators and principals have access only to aggregated, anonymous statistical metrics (e.g., "8 consultations in 8th grade regarding anxiety"). They cannot access individual private session transcripts.

4. Full Offline Capability:

The Windows desktop application functions completely air-gapped without requiring an active internet connection, storing all records locally.

No. The Windows desktop app installs in under 2 minutes with zero configuration. The web version runs in any modern browser without extra software.

Yes. The problem taxonomy, session types, and multidisciplinary forms can be tailored for elementary, middle, high school, and vocational/higher education environments.
