Subject: Partnership Proposal: Open MHPSS Infrastructure for UNICEF (Digital Public Good)

Dear Colleagues,

I am writing to you as a fellow professional in the MHPSS field. My career in youth prevention spans over 25 years and includes field research on adolescent deviance for UNICEF and WHO, as well as serving as a manager and analyst for the UNODC-supported ESPAD youth surveys.

Culminating this professional journey, I have engineered the architecture of Prevention-AI (prevention.school)—an AI-native public health ecosystem for government and educational organizations working with minors. It resolves a set of critical systemic bottlenecks:

  • Provides a ready-to-use, scalable CRM system that unites the electronic workspaces of all participants within the youth deviance prevention system;
  • Eliminates the epidemiological data gap by integrating dedicated applications for parents, teenagers, educational institutions, and public authorities. Furthermore, the platform bridges the public and private sectors through an application suite tailored for commercial psychological centers;
  • Overcomes the well-known limitations of classical psychodiagnostic methods (such as low engagement or response distortion) through "Shadow Scoring" technology. The AI analyzes the user's natural free-flowing speech during empathetic dialogue and programmatically maps it onto strict clinical metrics, preserving the scientific validity of the assessment;
  • Builds capacity across all the aforementioned specialist categories through an integrated educational course on health psychology and youth prevention program design;
  • Automatically generates comprehensive statistical pools detailing the activities of school psychologists using the Prevention-AI platform (analytics that standard departmental reporting is fundamentally unable to provide);
  • Creates an "Evidence Factory" for AI-mediated psychological support through continuous aggregation, structuring, and analysis of big data, as well as ongoing evaluation of the AI assistance protocols;
  • Establishes a territorial system for youth deviance prevention at any scale via a hierarchy of dashboards and cross-cutting metrics—from an individual school psychologist up to the national or UN level.

It is crucial to emphasize that the entire core infrastructure for public schools and state organizations is designed to be distributed absolutely free of charge (as a Digital Public Good). Paid options are entirely voluntary and apply exclusively to advanced AI subscriptions for the commercial sector or private users. This ensures the project's financial sustainability without placing any burden on public education budgets.

The core architecture of the platform—including the family application and specialist terminals—is fully developed and operational. I am writing to propose a conceptual partnership: bringing the first MHPSS system built on the separation of open infrastructure and a strictly moderated Cognitive Core to the global stage.

My goal is to launch the interfaces and CRM (Terminals) as a completely free Digital Public Good (DPG). To demonstrate absolute transparency, I have already made this source code publicly available for your technical team's review: https://github.com/RomanDubrovsky/prevention-terminal-dpg. Any school in the world will be able to deploy the system and manage electronic records at no cost.

However, the highest quality of specialist support is driven by the platform's Cognitive Core (AI Brain)—a complex hierarchy of clinical prompts, protocols, and RAG architecture that acts as an expert co-pilot. Unlike the UI, this Brain cannot be released into unregulated Open Source. It requires strict centralization, clinical oversight, and continuous development to mitigate safety risks.

The global architecture of the Cognitive Core and its scientific foundation are detailed publicly at https://prevention.school/science/. The closed repository itself, containing the algorithmic hierarchy and clinical prompt library (the AI Brain), remains protected. Should UNICEF express a strategic interest in governing this architecture, I would be delighted to provide your technical and scientific teams with full access to the closed repository for a deep audit of the platform and to discuss the mechanics of transferring operational control.

I am a researcher and full-stack architect. I am proposing that UNICEF act as the institutional partner specifically to develop and oversee this Cognitive Core. Your resources would enable the localization of the protocol base for diverse cultural contexts, the scaling of AI computational capacity, and, most importantly, the enforcement of rigorous ethical control over the AI's operational logic.

To ensure total compliance with DPG principles, I deliberately self-funded the platform's development. My intellectual property is free from venture capital obligations, allowing for a clean transfer of operational control over the project without legal complications.

The platform and its applications are available in a fully functional state on my website: https://prevention.school/
