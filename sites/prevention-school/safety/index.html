AI Safety, Ethics & Safeguards Framework

Standards Alignment: Common Sense Media & NIST AI Risk Management Target Audience: Schools, Specialists & Oversight Bodies

1. Ethical AI Manifesto for Youth & Mental Well-being

Artificial Intelligence in educational and psychological settings must operate under higher standards than consumer chatbots. At Prevention.school, we adhere to a non-negotiable principle: Technology should empower human connection, never isolate or deceive vulnerable individuals.

2. Core Safety Pillars

Pillar 1: Strict Human-in-the-Loop (No Autonomous Clinical Decisions)

Our AI engines function solely as administrative co-pilots and methodological structuring tools. The AI drafts documentation, flags risk markers, and suggests evidence-based psychoeducational materials, but never issues independent psychological diagnoses, disciplinary sanctions, or medication advice without certified specialist verification.

Pillar 2: Zero-Hallucination Deterministic Protocols

Unlike open-ended generative chat systems that can invent harmful recommendations, all preventative workflows in Prevention.school follow deterministic, validated psychological methodologies (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Evidence-Based Psychological First Aid, and validated screening inventories). Prompts are bound to strict JSON schema validation and clinical ontology trees.

Pillar 3: Real-Time Crisis Triaging & Hard Safety Boundaries

Any detection of explicit crisis signals (such as self-harm intentions, violence, sexual abuse, or acute psychosis) immediately suspends standard conversational flows and triggers emergency escalation protocols, directing the user to verified local human hotlines and notifying designated school crisis coordinators.

Pillar 4: Zero-Knowledge Privacy Architecture

We do not store identifiable student records on central cloud servers. Consultations and records are secured via local encryption on the practitioner's device or strictly isolated institutional databases compliant with FERPA, COPPA, and GDPR.

3. Continuous Independent Evaluation

We welcome audits from non-profit digital wellness organizations, academic institutions, and child protection bodies. Inquiries regarding our model safety evaluation benchmarks can be directed to admin@prevention.school.
