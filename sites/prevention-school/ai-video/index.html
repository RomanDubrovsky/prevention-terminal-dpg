Four Pillars of the Educational Clinical Cockpit

Continuous clinical support before, during, and after student sessions and parent-teacher webinars

1

Session Mind Map & 4-Phase Counseling Sprints

Direct the counseling vector in real time. Structure sessions into 4 structured phases: 1. Exploration (identifying school triggers) → 2. Desensitization (emotional regulation) → 3. Cognitive Shift (reframing beliefs about competence/peers) → 4. Micro-Step (behavioral contract / home-school task).

🎯 1-Click Statuses: Mark topics as Active Sprint, Overcome/Insight, Homework, or Parking Lot with full two-way notes synchronization.
2

Deep Incongruence & Bullying Watcher

Identifies masked psychological defenses in youth: defensive laughter during distress ("it was funny when they took my backpack"), intellectualization, withdrawal, and sudden topic evasion.

⚡ Early Warning: Non-intrusive clinical alerts highlighting potential unspoken trauma or peer victimization.
3

After Call: 3 Documents in 30 Seconds

Upon finishing the call, the system transforms the dialogue into three essential educational deliverables:

📋 1. Student Record: Official school counseling log & behavioral notes.
🧠 2. Clinical Supervision: Protocol adherence score & growth areas.
💌 3. Parent Follow-up: Constructive, supportive guidance note for parents.
4

Interactive Webinar & Assembly Studio

Conduct interactive parent-teacher assemblies, class prevention workshops, and educator webinars with live poll screening, anonymous Q&A, and presenter slide decks.

🎙️ Class & Parent Engagement: Gauge parental consensus and class psychological climate in real time.
