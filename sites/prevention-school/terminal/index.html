Data Sovereignty

Zero-Cloud Airgapped Infrastructure

Patient identifiers, clinical notes, and institutional data reside exclusively within your physical operational perimeter.

The workspace functions as an isolated digital vault. Access is secured via stringent on-device credentialing and local SQLCipher encryption.

  • GUID-Based Tokenization: Students are represented internally via mathematically irreversible GUIDs. The AI analyzes behavioral vectors, not names or identities, ensuring absolute anonymity.
  • Zero-Knowledge AI Inference: Personally identifiable data never leaves the specialist's device. All processing occurs locally or is completely anonymized before interacting with the AI engine.
  • Offline Resilience: The core application and entire database remain fully operational without internet connectivity, ensuring uninterrupted care during network outages.
  • Secure Redundancy: Practitioners can export encrypted, password-protected database backups to secure physical media. These backups are unreadable on unauthorized devices.
  • Compartmentalized Auditing: Institutional administrators can extract macro-level capacity and risk heatmaps without ever accessing individual clinical records.
