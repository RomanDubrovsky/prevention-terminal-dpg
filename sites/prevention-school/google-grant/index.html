3. Problem Statement & Market Opportunity

Google Application Prompt:
What problem is your startup solving, and what is the market opportunity?

Modern mental healthcare and adolescent prevention face a catastrophic triple bottleneck:

  1. The School Specialist Bottleneck: School and frontline psychologists spend over 80% of their working hours on administrative reporting and paperwork, leaving less than 20% for direct student support. Overwhelmed specialists lack automated tools to detect distress early.
  2. The Parent-Teen Trust Crisis: Existing family tech is split between intrusive "parental spy apps" (which destroy trust) and unstructured, hallucination-prone consumer chatbots that lack clinical safety guardrails and crisis triage.
  3. The Prevention Data Gap: Public health authorities, schools, and employers operate blindly without real-time, privacy-preserving epidemiological telemetry to allocate support resources before crises escalate into psychiatric hospitalization, dropout, or corporate burnout.
Market Opportunity:

The global digital mental health and corporate wellbeing market exceeds $26B, while global public health and school MHPSS initiatives (WHO HPS / UNESCO / UNICEF) actively demand open-standard, scalable Digital Public Goods. By bridging the public sector (free DPG) with commercial clinics and corporate wellness (B2B SaaS), Prevention AI unlocks a high-impact, self-sustaining multi-sided market.
