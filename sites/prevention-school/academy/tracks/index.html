Mindful Use of AI

In the age of artificial intelligence, it is tempting to delegate every task to algorithms. However, thoughtless use of even the most sophisticated tools does not make someone a specialist.

Our Academy was created to give you a fundamental understanding of health psychology and prevention. Training is essential so that you consciously govern the Terminal platform, understand the logic behind recommendations, and make sound professional decisions.

“Artificial intelligence is a powerful assistant, but strategy, empathy, and the final decision always belong to the professional. Understanding the underlying methodology is what makes you an expert.”
